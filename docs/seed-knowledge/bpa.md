# Bisphenol A: seed knowledge for the screening and extraction agents

This file is read by the literature screener before it reads a paper about bisphenol A. It is instructions for a reader, not evidence, and it is never shown on the site.

## Identity

- Bisphenol A, BPA, 4,4'-(propane-2,2-diyl)diphenol, 2,2-bis(4-hydroxyphenyl)propane. CAS 80-05-7.
- Bisphenol S (BPS), bisphenol F (BPF), bisphenol AF (BPAF), bisphenol B, bisphenol AP, bisphenol C and bisphenol Z are different compounds. A paper about one of them that names BPA only as background, as a comparator in a sentence, or in a list of analogues is not a BPA paper. A paper that measures BPA alongside analogues and reports BPA on its own is a BPA paper.
- BPA glucuronide and BPA sulfate are conjugated metabolites and are generally inactive at the estrogen receptor. A paper on conjugates alone is about exposure measurement unless it reports a reproductive outcome.
- "Bisphenol" or "bisphenols" without the letter usually means the class. Treat the paper as BPA only when BPA itself is analysed.

## What the reproductive domain covers

Reproductive endocrinology and reproductive outcomes in either sex: fertility and fecundability, semen quality and sperm parameters, testis and Leydig or Sertoli cell function, steroidogenesis and sex steroid levels (testosterone, estradiol, progesterone, LH, FSH, AMH, SHBG), ovarian reserve, folliculogenesis and oocyte quality, granulosa cells, polycystic ovary syndrome, endometriosis, uterine and endometrial endpoints, implantation and pregnancy outcomes that reflect reproductive function, puberty timing and the hypothalamic-pituitary-gonadal axis, assisted-reproduction outcomes such as IVF fertilisation and embryo quality.

Not the reproductive domain for screening purposes: neurodevelopment of the offspring, birth weight alone, metabolic or thyroid endpoints, cancers other than testicular or ovarian, dental and material science, plant and soil organisms, analytical chemistry and migration or packaging studies with no reproductive measurement.

## What the three search purposes mean

- Systematic reviews: the queue wants systematic reviews and meta-analyses of BPA and a reproductive endpoint. Narrative reviews, scoping reviews without a systematic method, and bibliometric analyses are not systematic reviews.
- Human reproductive: the queue wants studies in people, including cohort, case-control, cross-sectional, IVF clinic and pregnancy studies. Animal, in vitro and in silico work is excluded from this queue even when it is good work, because the queue exists to find human evidence.
- Contradictory or null: the queue wants any study of BPA and a reproductive endpoint that reports no association, no effect, or a result that contradicts the main literature. A hit here is still a BPA reproductive paper first; the search phrase "no effect" may have matched something unrelated.

## Reading habits that matter

- A title alone rarely settles an include. Without an abstract, only exclude when the title is decisive, otherwise ask for full text.
- Mixture studies (phthalates plus BPA, "endocrine disruptors") count when BPA is an analysed exposure, not when it is one word in a list.
- Mechanistic cell and animal work on BPA and steroidogenesis, germ cells or the gonadal axis is reproductive evidence for the contradictory and systematic-review queues, and is excluded from the human queue as animal-only or in-vitro-only.
- A retracted paper is never included.
